## THE VIRAL MATH

Traditional funnel:
- Pay to acquire customer → customer buys → done

Jeffy funnel:
- 1 person creates want → shares to get 10 → those 10 see other wants → some create their own → repeat

**You don't need 1000 people to see an ad. You need 10 people who actually want something.**

If 5 wants hit 10 verifications each = 50 verified humans
If 10% of those create their own want = 5 new wants
Each needing 10 more people...

The system compounds. Your job is just to start it.

## IF IT'S WORKING

Signs of traction:
- Strangers creating wants (not just your network)
- Comments asking "is this legit?"
- People sharing wants you didn't create
- Zone Partner applications coming in

**Then** you can pour fuel on with paid ads — but only to amplify what's already burning.

---

## IF IT'S NOT WORKING

After 2 weeks, if no want hits 10:
- The friction is too high (email verification too annoying?)
- The product selection wasn't compelling enough
- The value prop isn't landing

**Fix:** Talk to people who started but didn't finish. Ask why.
