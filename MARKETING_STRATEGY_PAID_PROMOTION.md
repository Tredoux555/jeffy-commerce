# JEFFY WANTS SYSTEM: Paid Promotion Strategy for South Africa

*Created: January 2, 2026*

---

## THE CORE PROPOSITION

**What we're selling:** A new way to buy from China — community-powered sourcing.

**The promise:** You tell us what you want. Get 10 friends to agree. We source it from China at real prices.

**Why it works:** South Africans are paying 3-5x markup on products that cost nothing in China. They know it. They're frustrated. Jeffy lets them fight back — together.

---

## PHASE 1: VALIDATION (Weeks 1-2)

**Goal:** Test messaging, find what resonates, build initial pixel data

**Budget:** R150/day = R2,100 for 2 weeks

### Platform: Facebook + Instagram only

Why not TikTok yet:
- FB/IG have better conversion tracking
- SA's 25-45 demographic (disposable income) still lives on Facebook
- Need pixel data before scaling

### Campaign Structure

**Campaign A: Problem-Aware (60% of budget)**

These people KNOW they're getting ripped off. Speak to that anger.

Ad Set 1: "The Markup Rant"
- Hook: "You're paying R800 for something that costs R90 in China."
- Body: Show price comparison (SA retail vs actual China price)
- CTA: "Fight back. Tell us what you want."
- Target: Online shoppers, Takealot buyers, bargain hunters, 25-45

Ad Set 2: "The Group Buy"
- Hook: "What if 10 people could buy together and skip the middleman?"
- Body: Explain the concept simply — you + friends = direct from factory
- CTA: "Start a group want"
- Target: Same as above + Facebook group admins, community leaders

**Campaign B: Product-Specific (40% of budget)**

Test 3-4 product categories to find winners:

Ad Set 1: Kitchen/Home
- "That R600 air fryer? R180 in China. Get 10 friends. We'll source it."

Ad Set 2: Beauty/Cosmetics  
- "Paying Clicks prices for products made in China? There's another way."

Ad Set 3: Electronics/Gadgets
- "Phone accessories at 1/5th the price. You just need 9 friends who agree."

Ad Set 4: Kids/Toys
- "Stop paying toy store markup. Source directly."

### Creative Format Priority

1. **Static image with price comparison** (easiest to test)
2. **Carousel: SA price → China price → How it works**
3. **Short video (15-30s)** showing the flow

### Targeting Details

**Core Audience:**
- South Africa (all provinces, but weight toward Gauteng, Western Cape, KZN)
- Age: 25-45
- Language: English (can test Afrikaans later)

**Interest Stacking:**
- Online shopping OR Takealot OR Shein OR AliExpress
- AND (bargain hunting OR deals OR sales)
- Exclude: People who work in retail/wholesale (competitors)

**Lookalike (Week 2):**
- Build from website visitors
- Build from want creators

---

## PHASE 2: OPTIMIZE (Weeks 3-4)

**Goal:** Double down on winners, kill losers, reduce CPA

**Budget:** R200-300/day based on Phase 1 results

### Actions

1. Kill any ad set with CPA > R50 (cost per want created)
2. Scale winning creative 2x
3. Create variations of winning hooks
4. Introduce retargeting

### Retargeting Campaigns

**Retarget A: Visited but didn't create**
- "Still thinking about it? Your want is waiting."
- Show social proof: "47 wants created this week"

**Retarget B: Created but didn't share**
- "Your [product] want needs 7 more people"
- "Share with friends who'd want this too"
- Direct link back to their want

**Retarget C: Verified but want isn't at 10 yet**
- "Almost there! 3 more people and we source it"

---

## PHASE 3: SCALE (Month 2+)

**Goal:** Predictable acquisition, expand platforms

**Budget:** R500+/day

### Add Platforms

**TikTok:**
- Younger demo, but growing purchasing power
- UGC-style content: "I just found out South Africans pay 5x markup..."
- Partner with micro-influencers (the 28 letters you've prepped)

**Google:**
- Search ads for "[product] South Africa cheap"
- "[product] from China"
- Catches intent-based traffic

**WhatsApp Click-to-Chat:**
- FB/IG ads that open WhatsApp
- Personal touch for high-intent users
- Zone Partners can handle conversations

### Influencer Integration

Use your 28 prepped letters:
- Micro-influencers (5k-50k followers) as Zone Partners
- They promote their own referral links
- Commission on wants that convert to sales
- Authentic content > paid ads at scale

## TRACKING & METRICS

### Must-Have Before Launch

1. **Meta Pixel** installed on jeffy.co.za
2. **Custom Events:**
   - `WantCreated` - fires on successful want submission
   - `WantShared` - fires on share button click
   - `WantVerified` - fires on email verification
   - `WantReady` - fires when want hits 10 verifications

### KPIs by Phase

| Phase | Primary Metric | Target |
|-------|---------------|--------|
| 1 | Cost per Want Created | < R30 |
| 2 | Cost per Verified Want | < R50 |
| 3 | Cost per Ready Want (10 verifications) | < R150 |

### Secondary Metrics

- Click-through rate (CTR): aim for >1.5%
- Share rate: % of creators who share their link
- Viral coefficient: verifications per share

## RISKS & MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Wants created but never hit 10 | Email nurture sequence to remind sharing |
| High CPA | Test more hooks, narrow targeting |
| Friends don't verify (email friction) | Consider WhatsApp verification as backup |
| Competitors copy model | Speed to market, Zone Partner network as moat |
