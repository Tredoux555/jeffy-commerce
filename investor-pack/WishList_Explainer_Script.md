Here's how the Jeffy Wish List works — and it couldn't be simpler.

Is there something you've always wanted? A gadget, an appliance, something for your home or your kids? Tell us. Go to jeffy.co.za, make your wish, and submit it. That's it — no buying, no catch.

And we don't do it quietly. Every winner is announced on the radio, in the newspaper, and across social media — a real celebration, with the moment your wish comes true shared for everyone to see.

The more wishes you submit, the more chances you have.

So go ahead — make a wish. Jeffy. Commerce that builds schools.
