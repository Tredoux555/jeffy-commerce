# Jeffy Commerce — Game Plan

The build-out plan for an import-and-supply distributor network, demand-led, funded for a hard-and-wide launch.

## The model (locked)
Jeffy imports → wholesales stock to a national network of **independent sellers** (each their own registered sole proprietor) on a **buy-sell** basis (they buy/own the stock, resell at a margin). Customers order on **jeffy.co.za**; Jeffy collects payment centrally via **PayFast Split Payments** (auto-splits each sale: Jeffy's wholesale cut + seller's margin). Sellers store stock in their garage and deliver locally, one run a day. Jeffy is VAT-registered (recovers import VAT). Think: **Takealot, but sellers deliver from their garage.**

Why it's clean: sellers own their stock = genuine independent businesses, not employees (no PAYE/min-wage/leave). VAT handled once, at wholesale. PayFast does the splitting, so Jeffy never illegally holds others' money.

## Phase 0 — Foundations (Weeks 1–2, run in parallel)
- **VAT registration** for Jeffy (unlocks import-VAT recovery).
- **Attorney drafts ONE pack:** Independent Reseller Agreement (buy-sell, credit terms, retention of title, independent-contractor, non-exclusive) + Wish List competition T&Cs. Model the agreement on Avon's.
- **Finish platform fixes:** rotate the leaked Supabase key; confirm green build; PayFast keys in.
- **Accounting live:** Xero + Nedbank feed; chart of accounts loaded.
- **Lock the landed-cost model** with real numbers (HS codes/duty, freight, FX) so pricing is truthful.

## Phase 1 — Demand discovery: the Wish List (Weeks 2–6)
- **Relaunch the existing "Wants" feature** as the Wish List (the bones are already built).
- **Structured capture:** product + category + price they'd pay + buy-frequency + location.
- **Weekly "wish granted" giveaway** (budget R100–200k), filmed and pushed on social; winners make their own unboxing content.
- **Drive participation** via social + early distributor recruits.
- **Analyse:** rank by repeat demand (not one-off wishes), weight by price-point and region.
- **Output:** shortlist of the **top ~100 sourceable products**.

## Phase 2 — Source & prove (Weeks 4–8, overlaps Phase 1)
- Run top candidates through the **1688 sourcing system**; get real landed cost + duty per item.
- Filter wishes against what's actually **sourceable + profitable** at your price point.
- Order a **small test batch** of the top winners — prove they sell *before* the big capital goes in.

## Phase 3 — Network launch (Weeks 6–12)
- Recruit the **first distributor cohort** in the 1–2 areas with the hottest wishlist demand.
- Onboard: sign agreement, set **credit limit**, seed stock **on credit**.
- Turn on **geo-routing** (order → nearest seller) and **split payments**.
- Prove the **daily delivery loop** and the **unit economics** in those areas.

## Phase 4 — Go wide (Month 3+)
- Deploy the **big investor capital** into proven top-100 winners — by the container.
- Roll distributors out **town-by-town, suburb-by-suburb**.
- Keep the **Wish List running permanently** as a live demand radar — it keeps telling you what to source next.

---

## The financial discipline (non-negotiable)
- Don't unleash the R5–6m until demand + unit economics are proven in a couple of areas first. Let the data aim the cannon.
- Every product priced off **true landed cost** (the model), never gut feel.
- **Per-distributor ledger:** stock out / sold / owed / paid / credit limit — your money is in their garages, so this is the heart of the system.
- Two-tier margin: your wholesale cut + their retail margin, both healthy.

## Investor angle
The Wish List delivers **fast, visible traction** (signups, buzz, content, hard demand data) cheaply — momentum to show the investor early — while the disciplined sourcing protects their capital from dead stock.

## Must be lawyer-blessed before signing sellers
1. Reseller agreement makes sellers genuine independent businesses (not employees).
2. "Jeffy collects on the seller's behalf" payment-agent setup documented so VAT stays on the wholesale leg only.
3. Branded network doesn't trip SA franchise law (avoid by charging no joining fee).
4. Wish List competition complies with CPA promotional-competition rules.
