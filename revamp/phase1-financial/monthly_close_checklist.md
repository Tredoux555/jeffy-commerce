# Monthly Close Checklist — Jeffy Commerce

Target: books closed and a management P&L produced by **day 5–7** of the following month. Tick in order.

## 1. Bank (Nedbank)
- [ ] Nedbank direct feed pulled into Xero; every line matched/coded.
- [ ] Zero unreconciled transactions on acc 1307614477.
- [ ] SWIFT/TT fees on CNY supplier payments coded to 440.

## 2. Sales & PayFast
- [ ] Gross sales (Xero 200) tie to storefront order total for the month.
- [ ] PayFast payouts reconciled: **gross sales − payouts = fees**. Book fees to 420.
- [ ] PayFast clearing account (615) nets to ~0 (only genuine in-transit at month end).
- [ ] Refunds processed and coded to 430.

## 3. Inventory & COGS (the importer-critical part)
- [ ] Each shipment received this month capitalised at **full landed cost** (model 310–340 → Inventory 630).
- [ ] COGS released for units sold (units sold × landed cost/unit).
- [ ] Goods-in-Transit (640) updated for stock still on the water.
- [ ] Supplier deposits (650) cleared when shipments land.
- [ ] Physical stock count vs Xero stock-on-hand reconciled; variance → 350.

## 4. FX
- [ ] CNY supplier payments revalued; FX gain/loss booked to 360.

## 5. Accruals & prepayments
- [ ] Ad spend, software, shipping-in-transit accrued if invoiced-not-paid.
- [ ] Annual prepayments (domain, software) apportioned.

## 6. Reporting
- [ ] Management P&L produced (use the **Monthly P&L** sheet in `Jeffy_Financial_Model.xlsx`).
- [ ] Contribution-margin + KPI dashboard updated (landed COGS/unit, CM%, CAC, break-even ROAS, DIO, cash-conversion cycle, runway).
- [ ] Variance vs prior month + vs plan reviewed.

## 7. Compliance calendar check
- [ ] Provisional tax (IRP6) dates — twice yearly.
- [ ] CIPC annual return month (+ Beneficial Ownership declaration).
- [ ] VAT201 — **N/A until registered** (watch the R2.3m / 12-month trailing turnover; voluntary option at R120k).
- [ ] Records filed: SAD500, commercial invoices, bills of lading, HS-code proof, FX advices (retain 5 years).

## 8. Cash
- [ ] Cash runway recalculated (weeks of cash at current burn).
- [ ] Next reorder funded? (Respect the 30–45 day sea-freight cash cycle — order before stock runs out.)
