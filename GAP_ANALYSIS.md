# JEFFY SYSTEM - GAP ANALYSIS
# Vision vs Built - December 27, 2025

## YOUR COMPLETE VISION:

### 1. JEFFY WANTS (Viral Engine)
- User submits want with image/link
- Shares on WhatsApp to get 10 agrees
- At 10 agrees → product added to catalog
- Creator gets FREE product + 10 to sell to cover cost

### 2. ZONE PARTNER DELIVERY SYSTEM
- Partners sign up (influencers or anyone)
- Keep ~10 of each item in stock
- 50% profit on delivered orders
- Print labels with unique QR codes
- Scan QR when packing → buyer gets WhatsApp "on the way"
- Buyer enables GPS for smooth delivery
- Route optimization for daily deliveries
- Buyer scans QR to confirm receipt
- Alternative: photo proof with geolocation
- One attempt per day, move on if difficult

### 3. CHINA AGENT PROCUREMENT
- Notification when product sells
- Weekly air freight shipments
- Stock replenishment based on sales

### 4. NO OVERHEAD MODEL
- No employees (zone partners are independent)
- No corporate rent
- No delivery fees (built into profit split)

The platform works for:

But lacks the "magic" connections:
- QR scanning workflow (the handoff moments)
- WhatsApp automation (the communication)
