## The agreed story (use this framing everywhere)

**Hook for non-SA / Chinese audiences:** "like China's community group-buying (社区团购), but the supply line runs straight from Chinese factories to South African households." The local seller = a group-buy leader (团长). Do **not** use "Takealot" for foreign audiences.

**The model (locked — matches `JEFFY_PROGRAM.md` §3):**
- Jeffy imports direct from China under its own SA importer's code; customers browse + **buy + pay on jeffy.co.za** (make the buy-on-platform step explicit — earlier drafts buried it).
- A **local seller** in the customer's own town delivers same-day from home.
- Sellers are **independent resellers** (buy-sell, Avon/Tupperware): buy wholesale, keep margin; first batch can be nothing-down / on credit (retention of title), then graduate to buying upfront. PayFast splits each sale in real time.
- **Wish List** = the engine: 1–10 wishes per person, weekly random draw, winner sourced + delivered free, celebrated on radio/newspaper/social. It is both the marketing event AND the live demand map that tells us what to stock.
- Two "smart parts" to always lead with: (1) customers tell us exactly what to stock (demand map → minimal dead stock); (2) no delivery fees + no corporate overhead.

**Naming decisions:**
- Call them **"sellers" / "resellers"** — NOT "agents." The independent-contractor (no PAYE) structure depends on it.
- Earlier chat sketch of a "free starter pack → 50/50 profit split" was the **retired** zone-partner model — do not reuse it. The buy-sell reseller model is canonical.
- **Schools / Foundation mission stays OUT** of public/marketing materials (internal only, per `JEFFY_PROGRAM.md` §2).

**3-year shape (plain version):**
- **Year 1 — prove it.** R50k first shipment of Tredoux's own trusted products (camping chair, S&P grinder, M128 bow, mobile battery compressor, magnetic kitchen strip, knives, archery releases, spotlights, gym gloves); one flagship seller (likely JHB) runs the whole loop to find/fix bugs; uni-student promotions manager (~R100k/yr, contractor, monthly invoice). Small prizes only; can start with a slower draw cadence then move to weekly.
- **Year 2 — grow carefully.** ~R1m, staged. Wish List intensified + feeds sourcing. Recruit local sellers in hottest-demand areas in **gated waves: 10 → 40 → 100**, widening only on proven sales + clean books. Cap stock risk (small/capped first batches, retention of title, replenish only after payment, hold a cash reserve).
- **Year 3 — profitable.** Good sellers reorder on their own; keep adding where data says. Watch per-seller monthly sales (illustrative break-even ~5–6 units/seller/month — replace with real landed-cost numbers).
