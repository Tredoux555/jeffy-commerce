## 2. YOUR ZONE

### What You Get

- **Exclusive territory** — GPS-defined boundaries, in writing
- **All orders in your zone come to you** — customers can't choose another ZP
- **Protection** — we don't allow overlapping partners, ever

### What You Don't Get

- **Ownership** — the zone is licensed to you, not sold to you
- **Permanence** — breach the terms, lose the zone
- **Transferability** — you can't sell or give away your zone without Jeffy's approval

### Zone Rules

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Only operate in YOUR zone | Other ZPs paid for their territory too |
| Don't poach customers from other zones | Kills trust in the whole network |
| Don't sell to resellers/other businesses | You're retail, not wholesale |

**If a customer contacts you from another zone:** Tell them to order through Jeffy. We'll route it correctly. Don't be clever — we track delivery addresses.

## 3. IF YOU WANT OUT

**Leaving on good terms (no debt):**

1. Tell us in writing (WhatsApp is fine)
2. Stop taking new stock immediately
3. Sell what you can over 14 days
4. We buy back unopened stock at **70% of wholesale**
5. Your deposit returns in full
6. Zone released, we part as professionals

**Leaving with outstanding balance:**

1. Deposit applied to what you owe
2. Remaining debt must be settled before buyback
3. No buyback until we're square

**Why 70% buyback?**

We have to redistribute or resell your stock. That costs time and money. 70% is fair — you recover most of your investment, we cover our costs.

Or sell it yourself before you exit — keep 100%.
