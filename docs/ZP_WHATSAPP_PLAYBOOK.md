# STAGE 1: FIRST CONTACT

## When they say: "Hi, I'm interested in becoming a Zone Partner"

### Response 1A - Warm Welcome
```
Hey! 👋 Thanks for reaching out.

Quick intro: I'm Tredoux, founder of Jeffy.

Zone Partners buy products from us at wholesale prices and sell in their area. You keep all the profit above wholesale.

To see if it's a good fit, can I ask you 3 quick questions?
```

### Response 1B - If they found you through an influencer
```
Hey! 👋 Glad [INFLUENCER NAME]'s post caught your attention.

I'm Tredoux - I run Jeffy.

Quick version: You buy stock from us cheap, sell it in your area, keep the profit. We handle sourcing from China, you handle your community.

Mind if I ask a few questions to see if we're a match?
```

### Response 1C - If they're vague ("tell me more")
```
Sure thing! Here's the 30-second version:

🛒 You buy products from us at wholesale (example: R100)
💰 You sell at retail in your area (example: R199)
✅ You keep the R99 profit

We source quality products from China. You get exclusive rights to your zone - no competition from other partners.

Starting cost is R500 deposit + R2,500 first stock order. But you get 7 days to sell before paying for stock.

Interested? Let me ask a few questions to check if your area is available.
```

# STAGE 2: QUALIFICATION

## The 3 Questions (send one at a time, wait for response)

### Question 1 - Money
```
First up: Do you have R500 available for the refundable deposit?

(The R2,500 stock comes with 7-day payment terms - sell first, pay later)
```

**If YES:** Move to Question 2
**If NO:** See "Gentle Disqualification" below

### Question 2 - Transport
```
Perfect. Next: Do you have transport to do deliveries?

Doesn't need to be fancy - car, bakkie, scooter, even a bicycle for local deliveries works.
```

**If YES:** Move to Question 3
**If NO:** See "No Vehicle Script" below

### Question 3 - Area
```
Last one: Which area/township/suburb are you in?

I'll check if it's still available. We only allow one partner per zone so you don't compete with each other.
```

**Then:** Check if zone is taken (see Stage 3)

## DISQUALIFICATION SCRIPTS

### No R500 Available
```
No stress - I get it, timing matters.

Here's what I'd suggest: save up the R500 over the next few weeks, and message me when you're ready. I'll check if your area is still open.

In the meantime, you can see our products at jeffy.co.za 👍
```

### No Vehicle
```
Transport is important because customers expect delivery.

A few options:
• Partner with someone who has transport (split the work)
• Start with a bicycle for nearby deliveries
• Save up for a cheap scooter (R8-15k secondhand)

Message me when you've sorted transport and we'll pick this up!
```

### Area Already Taken
```
Ah, [AREA] already has a partner. Sorry about that!

Two options:
1. I can put you on the waitlist - if that partner leaves, you're first in line
2. Is there a nearby area you could cover instead? Sometimes adjacent zones work.

Let me know what you'd prefer.
```

### Red Flags (pushy, unrealistic expectations, sketchy vibes)
```
Thanks for your interest, but I don't think Jeffy is the right fit for what you're looking for.

Wishing you the best with your ventures! 🙏
```
