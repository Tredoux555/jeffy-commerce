## THE JEFFY VOICE

### Tone:
- Confident, not arrogant
- Direct, not aggressive
- Visionary, not preachy
- South African, not American-startup-speak

### Words to USE:
- Hustle, build, opportunity, community
- Factory-direct, 50/50, Zone Partner
- South African, local, your territory
- Purpose, mission, schools, future

### Words to AVOID:
- Disruption, pivot, synergy (startup clichés)
- Easy money, get rich quick, passive income
- Dropshipping, MLM, scheme
