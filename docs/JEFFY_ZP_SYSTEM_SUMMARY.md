# PART 1: THE BUSINESS MODEL

## What Is a Zone Partner?

A Zone Partner (ZP) is an independent business owner who:
- Buys products from Jeffy at **wholesale prices**
- Sells them in their **exclusive zone** at retail prices
- Keeps **all the profit** above wholesale

**They are NOT employees.** They are your wholesale customers with territory rights.

---

## The Legal Structure

```
JEFFY (Pty) Ltd
      │
      │  Wholesale Purchase Agreement
      │  (NOT a franchise, NOT employment)
      │
      ▼
Zone Partner (Independent Business Owner)
      │
      │  Owns the stock
      │  Sets their own prices
      │  Bears the risk
      │  Handles their own tax
      │
      ▼
End Customer
```

**Why this matters:**
- No franchise disclosure requirements (CPA)
- No employment law obligations (UIF, PAYE, leave)
- Simple buyer-seller relationship
- You're a wholesaler, they're a retailer

---

## Your Margin (Where You Make Money)

You don't charge fees. You mark up the product.

```
YOUR LANDED COST (from China):     R80
YOU SELL TO ZP AT:                 R95  (18% markup)
ZP SELLS TO CUSTOMER AT:           R180

YOUR GROSS PROFIT:                 R15/unit
ZP GROSS PROFIT:                   R85/unit
```

The ZP doesn't see your markup — they just see "wholesale price R95."

At scale:
- 50 ZPs moving 100 units/week each = 5,000 units/month
- Your margin: R15 × 5,000 = **R75,000/month gross**

## If They Want Out

**Clean exit (no debt):**
- 14 days written notice
- We buy back unopened stock at **70%** of wholesale
- Full deposit returned
- Zone released

**Why 70%?**
- You need margin to redistribute stock
- Discourages casual quitting
- Still fair — they recover most investment
- Or they can sell it themselves first (keep 100%)

---

## Termination Offenses

**Warning → Termination:**
- Late payment (3 times)
- Selling outside zone (repeated)
- Sharing wholesale prices publicly

**Immediate Termination:**
- Poaching another ZP's customers
- Fraud (fake orders, fake payments)
- Selling counterfeit as Jeffy
- Public reputation damage

# PART 6: AGENT RELATIONSHIP

## How Your Agent Makes Money

**No salary. No separate fees. Commission via shipping.**

```
YOUR ARRANGEMENT:
├── Agent sources from 1688 at your direction
├── Agent ships to JHB (bulk, sea freight)
├── Agent stores in his warehouse (no rent to you)
├── Agent picks/packs/delivers to ZPs
│
└── HIS CUT: Built into shipping costs
    └── He charges what he charges for logistics
    └── That's his margin
    └── Volume = more money for him
```

**Why this works:**
- His incentive is aligned with yours (more volume = more fees)
- No fixed cost to you when volume is low
- He has existing infrastructure (warehouse)
- Relationship built on mutual benefit

# PART 13: PHILOSOPHY

## The Core Principles

**1. Zones have value because they're protected.**
Every ZP knows no one else can operate in their territory. That's what they're paying for.

**2. Trust is earned, not given.**
New ZPs pay upfront. Proven ZPs get terms. VIPs get flexibility.

**3. Be the landlord, not the loan shark.**
Firm rules, fair enforcement. Communicate and we work with you. Ghost us and we replace you.

**4. Early adopters take risk, late adopters pay premium.**
Wave 1 founders get R5k zones that will cost R50k later. That's their reward for believing early.

**5. Volume is everyone's incentive.**
You make more with more ZPs. Agent makes more with more shipping. ZPs make more with more sales. Aligned incentives.

**6. Simple beats clever.**
WhatsApp > fancy apps. Spreadsheets > complex systems. Get 20 ZPs running before you build automation.
