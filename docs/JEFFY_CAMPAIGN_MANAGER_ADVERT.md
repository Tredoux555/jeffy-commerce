# Jeffy Wishlist Campaign — Manager Advert (student / side-hustle)

Target: a **media / journalism / film / comms student** who's phone-native at editing (CapCut), AI-current, and culturally fluent in SA. Framed as a **part-time side-hustle, not employment** — flexible, remote, project-paced. Post it on university channels + creator DMs.

> **Before posting:** set the pay line. Suggested student-side-hustle range **R5,000–R9,000/month part-time** (you can scale it as the work grows). Replace `[R___/month]` below.

---

## Version A — University career pages / LinkedIn / notice boards (full)

**Side-hustle: Social Storyteller for a South African startup with a heart (remote, part-time)**

We're Jeffy. Once a month we grant one real wish to an ordinary South African — and we film it, honestly and with dignity, then share the story. Behind it is a bigger mission: building toward free, merit-based schools for kids who deserve a shot.

We're looking for **one student** to be the eyes and voice of that — not as an employee, as a **paid side-hustle** you run around your studies.

**What you'd do**
- Run our WhatsApp "wish line" — read the entries, spot the stories worth telling.
- Shoot and cut short vertical video (CapCut is perfect) that doesn't look corporate.
- Post across Facebook, TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, and learn what makes people share.
- Use AI tools to work faster — we want someone already curious about that.

**You might be our person if you're**
- Studying **media, journalism, film, communications or marketing** (or just genuinely good at this).
- Phone-native with video — you already make content people actually watch.
- Emotionally switched-on — the wish line will carry heavy, real stories.
- Culturally fluent in SA; **isiZulu and/or isiXhosa is a big plus**.
- Self-driven — we're a small team managing remotely.

**The deal**
- Remote, flexible around your timetable. **[R___/month]**, part-time, to start — room to grow as the campaign grows.
- Real ownership of a brand from the ground floor.

**One non-negotiable:** every person we film gives consent first, we never make anyone perform gratitude for a camera, and we tell stories about people's *dreams*, never their poverty. If that matters to you the way it matters to us, we want to talk.

**To apply:** send your Instagram/TikTok or a reel you're proud of (portfolio over CV), and one line on why this matters to you → [your email / WhatsApp].

## Where to post it (university-first)

- **Department pages & student groups** — media/journalism/film/comms specifically: **Rhodes (JMS)**, **Wits**, **UJ**, **Stellenbosch / CPUT** (film & media), **UCT**, **DUT**, **TUT**. Email the department admin or post in their student WhatsApp/Facebook groups.
- **Campus career portals** — most have a student-jobs/side-hustle board; "part-time, remote" fits.
- **Instagram / TikTok directly** — search SA student creators already making emotional human-interest content and DM them Version B. The feed *is* the CV.
- **RecruitMyMom / student job boards** — for flexible remote workers.
- **Your own network** — a referral beats a cold hire for trust-heavy work.

**Screen with a paid trial** (don't skip): give 2–3 finalists 60s of footage from a real or mock granted wish → ask for one FB post, one TikTok cut, and the caption. Their output + whether they instinctively get the dignity rule tells you everything.
