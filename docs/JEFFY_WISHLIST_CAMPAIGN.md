## 0. THE ONE-LINE STRATEGY

> We do not buy reach. We manufacture reach by granting real wishes and turning each one into a story the internet shares for free.

Radio at SABC scale (R20k+ per 30-second spot, ~R5M/year for a real national campaign) is the **wrong tool to launch with**. It shouts once and disappears. A single granted wish, filmed properly, lives forever, gets reshared, and *becomes* the advertisement. Content is the engine. Radio, if used at all, is a **round-2 amplifier** — and ideally paid for by a corporate partner once the stories prove the model.

## 1. THE MECHANIC (keep it brutally simple)

Entry must cost the entrant almost nothing and must NOT require a smartphone or website visit. Our demographic is heavily on feature phones, prepaid data, and WhatsApp.

**Primary channel: WhatsApp line**
- Free for anyone with any data.
- **Voice-note friendly** — critical. Low-literacy entrants can simply *talk* their wish. This is a feature, not an afterthought.
- Auto-reply confirms entry and sets expectations (see script pack).

**Backup channel: Missed-call number**
- Entrant calls, hangs up, costs them R0. We SMS or call back with a simple prompt.

**The single question (do not add more):**
> "If we could grant one wish to change your life, what would it be — and why?"

The **"why" is the gold.** It is the story pipeline. Every "why" is a potential hero narrative.

**Cadence:** Grant **one wish per month**, filmed start to finish. 12 months = **12 hero stories** = a full year of anchor content, each spawning many clips.

---

## 2. POSTING FREQUENCY — THE ALGORITHM QUESTION (answered directly)

**Yes, daily posting is what the algorithm rewards — but with a critical nuance:**
- Consistency beats volume. The algorithm rewards accounts that show up *reliably*, not accounts that dump 7 mediocre posts then go quiet.
- **Target: 1 post/day minimum, 5–7 days/week, every platform.** But a strong 5/week beats a burnt-out, low-quality 7/week.
- The trick: **one filmed wish = 8–15 pieces of content.** Teaser → the entry/"why" → the surprise reveal → the reaction → behind-the-scenes → a 1-month-later follow-up → vertical cuts for each platform. One event, weeks of daily posts. This is how you hit daily cadence without daily *new* events.
- **Platform priority:**
  1. **Facebook** — PRIMARY. This is where lower-income South Africans actually live. Do not underweight it because it feels "old."
  2. **TikTok** — the free virality lottery. The algorithm can hand an unknown account millions of views if the emotion lands. Costs nothing but a phone and a true story.
  3. **Instagram Reels** + **YouTube Shorts** — repurpose the same vertical cuts. Near-zero extra effort.

## 3. THE TWO BUDGET SCENARIOS

### SCENARIO A — WITHOUT a dedicated manager (Tredoux/Bayan run it)
**~R5k–15k/month → R60k–180k/year**
- Lean, doable, but caps growth because consistency suffers when you're running everything else.

- Use this only as a fallback if hiring slips.

### SCENARIO B — WITH a dedicated manager (RECOMMENDED, fits the R200–420k envelope)
**~R15k–35k/month → R200k–420k/year**

This sits inside the approved envelope and is **under one-tenth** of the radio-only plan, while being far more effective. Twelve wishes become hundreds of content pieces, each a shot at viral reach money can't buy.

## 4. THE 12-MONTH ROADMAP

### PHASE 1 — PROVE IT (Months 1–3)
- Hire the manager (Month 0–1).
- Stand up the WhatsApp line + missed-call backup.
- **Launch with proof, not a request.** Pre-film and grant the FIRST wish *before* going public. Launch post = "Look what we just did for this family — you're next," NOT "send us your wishes." People enter when they believe it's real.
- Grant 1–2 wishes. Film raw and honest. Post to FB + TikTok + Reels + Shorts.
- Boost only what's already performing organically. R2–5k/month boost spend.
- **Goal:** Validate that the mechanic floods the WhatsApp line and that the story format moves people. Cheap learning before scaling.

### PHASE 2 — FIND THE FORMULA (Months 4–8)
- Double down on whatever video format performed best in Phase 1.
- Daily cadence locked in via the multi-cut method.
- Begin approaching **community radio** (campus + local stations, R150–R800/spot, NOT SABC). Many will run a feel-good Jeffy story *for free* as content because it's good for them too.
- Start a light corporate-partner outreach list (retailers, banks, telcos with CSI/CSR budgets).

### PHASE 3 — LEVERAGE (Months 9–12)
- Use the stack of 8–10 proven hero stories as the pitch deck to corporate CSI/CSR partners.
- The real unlock: a partner *funds the wishes and the granting* in exchange for brand association. Now radio (even paid Ukhozi spots) becomes affordable because someone else is paying — and it amplifies something already proven, not a gamble.
- Consider a "Year in Wishes" compilation as an annual flagship piece.

## 5. "HARD AND HOT" LAUNCH TACTICS

1. **Seed with one pre-granted wish.** Proof first. (See Phase 1.)
2. **Early wish selection optimises for shareability AND need** — a wish with a strong, visual, emotional arc gets shared, which brings the next 10,000 entrants for free. (Later, as volume grows, you can weight purely toward need.)
3. **Lean on Facebook + TikTok organic + tiny boosts.** Do not pay for reach you can earn.
4. **Hold radio as the round-2 weapon** — amplify a proven viral story, ideally on a partner's budget.

## 6. NON-NEGOTIABLE ETHICS (this protects the Jeffy/Isibani brand for decades)

This campaign lives or dies on **dignity**. Filmed poverty reads as either genuine upliftment or exploitative spectacle, and which one the public perceives attaches *permanently* to the Jeffy and Isibani names. Lock the tone before anything goes out.

- **Proper, recorded consent** for every recipient, before filming. Plain-language, in their own language.
- **Frame recipients as people with dreams**, not objects of pity. The story is about aspiration, not destitution.
- **NEVER make anyone perform gratitude for the camera.** No staged crying, no "say thank you to Jeffy." If the emotion is real it will show on its own; if it isn't there, don't fake it.
- Let people **keep their pride.** Dignity in framing, editing, and captions.
- Anyone can decline to be filmed and still receive their wish.

This is a hard rule for the manager and for every edit. It is in the manager's brief and in their contract.
