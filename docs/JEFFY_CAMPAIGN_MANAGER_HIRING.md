# JEFFY — HIRING THE CAMPAIGN / SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGER

This is **Step 1** of the Wishlist Campaign. Everything else waits on this.
Role: Remote, SA-based. Routes through Montree Commerce, paid via Montree Limited (HK) / Wallex during trial, same as your other SA hire.

---

## 1. WHO YOU'RE ACTUALLY LOOKING FOR

Not a generic "social media intern." You need a rare blend:

- **Storyteller first, marketer second.** They must instinctively understand that the emotion in the wish is the product.
- **Phone-native video editor.** Can shoot and cut vertical video that doesn't look corporate. CapCut-fluent.
- **Emotionally mature inbox manager.** The WhatsApp line will receive heartbreaking messages daily. They need to handle that with care and not burn out.
- **Culturally fluent in low-income SA.** Ideally speaks isiZulu and/or isiXhosa, understands township and rural realities, won't accidentally produce something that reads as exploitative.
- **Self-starting & remote-reliable.** You're managing from Beijing. They must run without daily hand-holding.

**Seniority tiers (choose against budget):**
- **R8k–12k/month:** Junior but hungry — strong content creator, you provide more direction. Good if you (Tredoux) stay close to strategy.
- **R12k–20k/month:** Mid-level — runs the whole content engine + inbox + light strategy independently. **The recommended target.**

---

## 2. WHERE TO FIND THEM

In rough order of signal quality:

1. **LinkedIn** — search "social media manager" / "content creator" + South Africa, filter for people who've run campaign or NGO/cause content. Look at their actual posts, not their CV.
2. **Instagram / TikTok itself** — find SA creators already making emotional, well-edited, human-interest content with real engagement. The portfolio IS the feed. DM them.
3. **OfferZen / RecruitMyMom / Pnet / Careers24** — SA remote-work pools. RecruitMyMom in particular surfaces capable, flexible remote workers.
4. **University comms/journalism grad groups** (Rhodes, Wits, UJ, DUT) — hungry, affordable, culturally sharp, often multilingual.
5. **Your own network** — post the role; referrals beat cold hires for trust-heavy work like this.

**Screening test (do this, don't skip):** Give 2–3 finalists a short paid trial brief — "Here's a (real or mock) granted wish, 60 seconds of footage. Produce one FB post, one TikTok cut, and write the caption." Their output tells you everything a CV can't, especially whether they get the dignity rule instinctively.

---

## 3. THE PITCH (use this to attract the right person)

> This is not a normal social media job.
>
> Jeffy exists to fund free, merit-based schools for South African kids who deserve a shot — starting on a family farm in KwaZulu-Natal, eventually across Africa. Before the schools, we're running something simpler and more human: the Wishlist.
>
> Once a month, ordinary South Africans tell us one wish that would change their life. We grant one. We film it — honestly, with dignity, never as a pity show — and we share the story.
>
> Your job is to be the voice and the eyes of that. You'll run our WhatsApp wish line, shoot and cut the videos, and build a following that turns one granted wish a month into a movement people can't look away from. You won't be making ads. You'll be making the kind of stories people send to their mom.
>
> It's remote. It's real. And it's the ground floor of something built to outlast all of us — planting trees under whose shade we'll never sit.
>
> If you can tell a true story without ever stealing someone's dignity, we want to talk to you.

**Tone note:** This pitch deliberately leads with mission, not money. The right candidate self-selects on meaning. The wrong ones won't bother — which is exactly what you want.

---

## 4. THE NON-NEGOTIABLE THEY MUST AGREE TO

Put this in the brief AND the contract. It's a filter:

- Every recipient gives recorded consent before filming.
- Recipients are framed as people with dreams, never objects of pity.
- No one is ever made to perform gratitude for the camera.
- Anyone can decline filming and still get their wish.

If a candidate doesn't immediately get why this matters, they're not the hire.

---

## 5. PRACTICAL ONBOARDING (once hired)

- **Payment:** Montree Commerce → Montree Limited (HK) via Wallex, trial period, same rails as your existing SA hire.
- **Tools they'll need:** WhatsApp Business, CapCut (free), a Meta Business account for FB/IG, a TikTok account, a shared Google Drive for footage.
- **Reporting:** weekly 30-min remote check-in + a simple shared tracker (entries received, wishes granted, top-performing post, spend).
- **First 30 days** = Phase 1 of the campaign plan: stand up the line, pre-grant + film wish #1, launch with proof.
