## WHY E-COMMERCE FAILS HERE

1. **Address Problem** - Houses "don't exist on maps," drivers waste 15+ min per delivery
2. **Data Costs** - R85/GB (6x emerging market average), 28% avoid phone use due to costs
3. **Trust Deficit** - 2017 SASSA scandal created lasting distrust of digital payments
4. **Payment Reality** - 65% withdraw over half monthly income for cash transactions

**Jeffy's Solution:** WhatsApp-first, cash-on-delivery, spaza pickup points, community agents
