# JEFFY COMMERCE
## Commerce That Builds Schools

---

### THE MISSION

Fund free, merit-based schools in South Africa through sustainable e-commerce.

---

### HOW IT WORKS

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                 │
│   CHINESE FACTORIES  →  JEFFY  →  ZONE PARTNERS  →  CUSTOMERS   │
│   (Factory prices)      (Platform)   (Local sellers)  (SA)      │
│                                                                 │
│   Zone Partners keep their profit margin                        │
│   Jeffy's net profit → 100% to schools                         │
│                                                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### ZONE PARTNER MODEL

Township entrepreneurs become local distributors:

- **Start with R500** → Receive R2,500 in stock
- **Exclusive zone** → No competition in your area  
- **Keep all profit** above wholesale price
- **WhatsApp support** from sourcing team in China

Example: Buy for R100, sell for R199 = R99 in your pocket, every sale.

---

### THE SCHOOLS VISION

**Merit-Only Selection**
- No fees, no connections, no wealth required
- Selected purely on potential and character

**What Graduates Receive**
- 1 hectare of land
- Skills to build their own home
- Training in practical production:
  - Agriculture & food production
  - Technology & manufacturing
  - Medicine & healthcare basics
  - Clothing & textiles

**Self-Sufficient Communities**
- Graduates can feed, clothe, house, and heal themselves
- Annual gatherings share innovations
- Model expands: SA → Africa → International
